About Bundoora Family Clinic

A secure web-based system designed to modernise prescription and medication record management.

Our purpose

Reducing paper, delays and medication-management errors.

The digital system centralises prescription creation, pharmacist verification, dispensing, medication tracking, inventory control, notifications and auditability for patients and clinic staff.

✓
Secure role-based access
Users only access modules appropriate to their role.
✓
Accountability
Critical actions are recorded in audit logs with timestamps and IP addresses.
✓
Accessible design
Responsive pages for desktop, tablet and mobile use.

System objectives

Electronically generate, sign, verify and dispense prescriptions; monitor stock and medicine batches; remind patients; and support reporting, safety and pharmacy coordination.
